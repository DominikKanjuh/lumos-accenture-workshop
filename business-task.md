# Case 2 — AI Strategy for Energy Client

An energy provider wants to know where AI can create real value across its business – from grid operations and trading to customer service.

## Your task
- Identify promising AI use cases along the energy value chain
- Choose an evaluation framework (e.g., value vs. feasibility)
- Assess and prioritize the use cases with your framework
- Recommend next steps for the top use cases

## What we look for
- Relevant, creative use cases
- A clear, well-justified framework
- Convincing prioritization and storytelling
